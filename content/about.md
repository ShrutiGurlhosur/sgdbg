I'm a malware analyst and reverse engineer. This site is where I publish detailed
write-ups of samples and campaigns I've dug into, along with the YARA rules and
automation scripts that come out of that work.

**What you'll find here:**

- **Malware analysis reports** — static and dynamic breakdowns of samples, from
  commodity loaders to targeted implants, with IOCs and detection guidance.
- **Threat intel notes** — observations on infrastructure, TTPs, and campaign
  tracking.
- **Techniques** — reverse engineering methodology, tooling walkthroughs, and
  notes on things that took me longer to figure out than they should have.
- **YARA rules** — a running library of detection rules, most of them tied to a
  specific writeup.
- **Scripts** — small automation tools I use for triage, unpacking, and
  extraction, usually with source on GitHub.

Everything here is published for defensive and educational purposes. IOCs and
detection content are intended for threat intelligence and detection engineering
use, not for offense.
